How NestGrow calculates
  • Contributions go in monthly, and returns compound monthly at the annual rate you choose.
  • "Max out" uses the 2026 IRS limits: 401(k) $24,500 (+$8,000 at 50+, or +$11,250 at ages 60–63), IRA $7,500 (+$1,100 at 50+), HSA $4,400 self-only / $8,750 family (+$1,000 at 55+).
  • Percentage match = the lesser of (your 401(k) contribution, salary × "up to" %) × match %. Your salary grows by the raise % each year.
  • "Contribute until age" means you contribute every year until you reach that age.
  • "Today's dollars" adjusts future values for the inflation rate you enter.
  • Results are pre-tax estimates for educational use only, not financial advice. Real returns vary year to year.
